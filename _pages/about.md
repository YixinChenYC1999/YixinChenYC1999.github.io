Yixin Chen received his B.E. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China, in 2021, and his M.S. degree in Computing Science from the University of Glasgow, Glasgow, UK, in 2022. He is currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Technology of the Department of Computer & Information Sciences at Towson University, MD, USA, advised by [Dr. Yifan Guo](https://yifan-guo.com/www/index.html) and [Dr. Wei Yu](https://wp.towson.edu/wyu/). His research interests include Edge AI System, Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and Cyber Security.
